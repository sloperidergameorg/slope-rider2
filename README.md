# ❄️ Slope Rider (2025) – Endless Snow-Riding Arcade Game

**Slope Rider** is an endless snow-riding arcade game where the goal is simple: survive as long as possible while sliding down an icy mountain slope. You control a sled that moves automatically downhill, navigating hazards and racing for high scores. Fast-paced gameplay, random slopes, and simple controls make every run feel fresh and exciting.

🎮 **Play for free online:** https://sloperidergame.org/

---

## 🧊 Gameplay Overview
- The sled moves forward automatically — your job is to steer and react  
- Survive as long as possible to increase your score  
- Randomly generated slopes keep each run unique  
- Fast pace and simple controls make the game easy to learn, hard to master

---

## 🪵 Obstacles
As you race down the mountain, you’ll encounter:
- Trees and pine trunks  
- Falling logs  
- Snowballs and boulders  
- Cracks or collapsing ground  
- Ice patches and cliffs

Every hazard demands quick reflexes!

---

## 🎮 Controls
- **Left / Right Arrow Keys:** Steer the sled  
- **Up Arrow Key:** Jump over obstacles

- Endless arcade gameplay
